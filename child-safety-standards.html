Child Safety Standards

Our Commitment to Protecting Children

InBlood is committed to the safety and protection of all users, especially children. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) content and behavior on our platform.

Our Commitment

InBlood is dedicated to creating a safe environment for all users. We recognize the critical importance of protecting children from exploitation, abuse, and harm. Our platform is designed with comprehensive safety measures and strict policies to prevent any form of child endangerment.

Prohibited Content and Behavior

InBlood strictly prohibits the following:

  • Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) content of any kind, including images, videos, text, or any other media that sexually exploits or endangers children.
  • Grooming behavior - any attempt to establish an emotional connection with a minor to lower their inhibitions for the purpose of sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Child trafficking - any content or behavior that facilitates or promotes the trafficking of children.
  • Sexualization of minors - any content that sexualizes children or presents them in a sexually suggestive manner.
  • Sharing or distribution of any content depicting child abuse or exploitation.
  • Solicitation of sexually explicit material from minors or attempts to engage minors in sexual conversations.

⚠️ Important: Any accounts found to be in violation of these standards will be immediately and permanently terminated from the InBlood platform. We take these violations extremely seriously.

Reporting and Enforcement

We take active measures to enforce these standards:

  • All reported CSAE content is immediately investigated by our trust and safety team.
  • Verified CSAE content is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement authorities as required by law.
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations involving child safety.
  • Users who violate these policies are permanently banned from InBlood.
  • We continuously monitor our platform for potential violations using both automated systems and human review.

User Safety Features

InBlood implements multiple safety features to protect all users, particularly minors:

  • Age verification systems to ensure users meet minimum age requirements (18+).
  • Robust reporting mechanisms available throughout the app.
  • Content moderation systems that review flagged content.
  • Privacy controls that allow users to manage their visibility and interactions.
  • Block and report features accessible from any user profile or conversation.

Our Responsibility

InBlood acknowledges our responsibility as a platform operator to:

  • Maintain and enforce policies that protect children from harm.
  • Provide clear mechanisms for reporting concerning content or behavior.
  • Act swiftly and decisively when violations are identified.
  • Cooperate with authorities to combat child exploitation.
  • Continuously improve our safety measures and policies.

Report a Concern

If you encounter any content or behavior on InBlood that you believe violates these Child Safety Standards, please report it immediately:

  • Use the in-app reporting feature (available on every profile and message)
  • Email us directly at: inblooddevelopment@gmail.com
  • For emergencies involving imminent danger to a child, contact local law enforcement immediately

All reports are treated confidentially and investigated promptly. We encourage our community to help us maintain a safe environment by reporting any concerning content or behavior.

Legal Compliance

InBlood complies with all applicable laws and regulations related to child safety, including:

  • 18 U.S.C. § 2258A (reporting requirements for CSAE)
  • Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy
  • International laws protecting children from exploitation
